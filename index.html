Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my uploaded files stored?
They aren't stored anywhere! DocuCraft processes your images and PDFs completely inside your browser locally using client-side JavaScript APIs. Your file details never interact with external servers.
Is there a file size or limit threshold?
No. Since processing happens natively using your computer's processing power rather than our cloud hardware resources, you can process as many documents as your system's hardware allows.
